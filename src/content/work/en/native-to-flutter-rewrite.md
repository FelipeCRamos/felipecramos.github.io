## Situation

A food-ordering platform was running on separate native iOS and Android apps. They had crashes, slow screens, poor error handling, a poor interface, confusing code and outdated frameworks. The decision was to replace both with a single Flutter application. The client’s name is withheld.

## Constraints

This was a complete rewrite of a product already in use. The top-down decision was to switch every user at once, so there was no gradual rollout to lean on.

## What I did

- Led the full rewrite and directed a team of 4 developers.
- Delivered the single Flutter application and moved all users to it in one switch.
- Stayed on as owner of the product through later releases.

## Result

The complete rewrite was delivered in under 6 months. Afterwards I moved to the same client’s native iOS product as a contributing engineer, returning to Swift 5 after a top-down stack decision.
