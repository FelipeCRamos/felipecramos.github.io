## Situation

Hurst Capital is a Latin American alternative-assets investment platform, offering fractional, tokenised exposure to assets such as judicial receivables, music royalties, real estate and art. I was the first engineer hired after a complete turnover of the mobile squad.

## Constraints

The codebase had no documentation, there was no handover, and nobody was left to ask.

## What I did

- Reverse-engineered the old code to understand what the app did.
- Defined a new architecture from scratch, then rebuilt the app module by module with better structure and code quality.
- Double-checked behaviour with the product team and the web system as I went.
- Owned mobile end to end, covering architecture, networking and analytics, with core flows in BLoC and RxDart over REST and a unit testing layer.
- Stood up analytics and attribution with Firebase, Adjust and Google Tag Manager.
- Ran the hiring process and brought on 3 engineers.

## Result

The MVP shipped in about 2 months. The business got its first view of mobile funnel behaviour. The data surfaced crashes caused by edge-case user behaviour, and showed which areas of the app were tapped and viewed most and where people spent their time. The delivery also earned room to grow the team. What started as one engineer with an unknown codebase became a built-out mobile squad.
